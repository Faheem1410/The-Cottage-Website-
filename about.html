Our story

Two businesses, one beautiful location

Newly opened in July 2026, The Cottage brings together two things we love under one roof: a relaxed daily brunch spot, and a venue built for life's biggest celebrations. We set out to bring something vibrant and new to Wednesbury and Birmingham — a fresh wedding hall and a fresh brunch spot, side by side.

Our main hall sets the stage for grand celebrations, with space for up to 350 guests — a genuinely grand canvas for your day.

Whether you're stopping in for a Full English on a Saturday morning, or planning a wedding for 350 guests, you'll find the same warm, cottage feeling either way.
